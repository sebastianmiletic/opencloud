# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who browse, track, and watch movies and television from a desktop or television-style interface. They use a mouse, keyboard, or directional remote and expect their account, collection, history, episode progress, and settings to follow them between devices.

## Product Purpose

Open Cloud combines discovery, watch tracking, and embedded playback in one focused desktop application. Success means users can find a title, continue the correct episode, start a healthy provider, and return later without losing progress or being interrupted by unsafe popups.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, restrained, and dependable. The interface should feel familiar and confident, with concise language and quiet feedback that does not compete with the content.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the app into a colorful dashboard, a collection of decorative cards, or an unfamiliar experimental player. Avoid changes that make existing mouse workflows slower, hide standard controls, or add motion without communicating state.

## Design Principles

- Content stays primary; controls appear where and when they are useful.
- Reliability is visible through honest loading, provider, blocker, and update states.
- New features preserve existing behavior and remain optional when they change playback policy.
- Keyboard, remote, pointer, and assistive-technology paths receive equal functional coverage.
- Progressive loading should make the app useful before every request has completed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the application shell. Support visible focus, logical tab order, directional keyboard and television-remote navigation, semantic labels, live status announcements, reduced motion, high contrast, and non-color-only health states while preserving normal pointer interaction.
